About Us

Stha's Bakery is a small local bakery based in Magojaneng, Kuruman, Northern Cape. We specialise in freshly baked biscuits in different flavours and soft, homemade scones made with care for every occasion.

Our products are suitable for individuals, families, ceremonies, unveiling events, funerals and other special occasions. Whether you need a small batch for tea time or a larger package for a gathering, we prepare orders to match your needs.

Everything is made in our kitchen using quality ingredients. We focus on consistent taste, friendly service and reliable collection so that every order feels personal. Many of our customers return for family events and recommend us to others in the community.

We are proud to serve Magojaneng and the wider Kuruman area. When you order from Stha's Bakery, you support a local business that puts heart into every batch of biscuits and scones.

More About Our Bakery

We are based in Magojaneng, Kuruman, in the Northern Cape, South Africa. We serve customers in the local area and surrounding communities. You can find us on the map on our Contact page.

All biscuits and scones are homemade and baked fresh. We offer flavours such as lemon, ginger and vanilla, and can prepare packages for ceremonies and special events. No mass production – each order is prepared with attention to detail.

Anyone – individuals ordering for home, families, and organisers of ceremonies, unveilings, funerals and other gatherings. We cater for both small personal orders and larger event packages.

Use the enquiry form on our Contact page, or call 081 678 4431 / email sthasbakery@gmail.com. We will confirm availability, flavours, quantities and collection arrangements for your order.

Orders are primarily collected from our base in Magojaneng, Kuruman. Please contact us to discuss collection times. For special arrangements, call or message us when you place your order.
