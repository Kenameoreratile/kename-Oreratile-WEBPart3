Our Services

We bake for everyday orders and for the important moments in your family’s life. From a small personal order to a full package for a ceremony or gathering, we prepare biscuits and scones with care.

Ceremonies

We provide baked products for different ceremonies and family gatherings. Tell us the number of guests and preferred flavours (lemon, ginger, vanilla or mixed), and we will prepare a suitable package for collection.

Special Events

Our products can be prepared for unveiling ceremonies, funerals and other events where homemade biscuits and scones are appreciated. We offer respectful, reliable service for sensitive occasions.

Individual Orders

Customers can order biscuits and scones for personal enjoyment, tea time or sharing with family and friends. Small batches are welcome – just let us know what you need and when.

Service Details

Contact us with the date, approximate number of guests and preferred products (scones, biscuits or a mixed package). We will confirm availability and prepare your order for collection from Magojaneng, Kuruman.

We understand these are sensitive occasions. We offer reliable, respectful service and can prepare suitable quantities of biscuits and scones for the day. Please order as early as possible so we can plan properly.

Please place larger orders as early as possible. For best results, contact us a few days before your event so we can plan baking and collection. Smaller personal orders may be arranged more quickly – call to check.

Orders are collected from our base in Magojaneng, Kuruman. We will confirm collection time when we accept your order. Use the map on our Contact page for directions.

Available flavours include lemon, ginger and vanilla biscuits, plus fresh scones. Event packages can mix products according to your preference. Mention any special requests in your enquiry.
